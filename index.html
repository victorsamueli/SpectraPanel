Prepare or Upload Your Database

Click Download Excel Template to get the clean, streamlined .xlsx workbook. Fill in your lab's primaries, secondaries, direct dyes, and reporter lines, then click Upload Database to import all sheets at once.

Browse & Select Targets (Left Table)

Switch between criteria tabs (Primaries, Dyes, Reporters). Filter by any column or search. Check the box next to any reagent to add it to your panel. Click 🔓 to lock a mandatory target into all combinations.

3

Configure Optical Channels & Generate

In the Microscopy Channel Configuration tab, set your lasers/filters with real-time physical spectrum color pickers. In the designer, check permitted channels and click Generate Combinations to view valid multiplex panels.

Custom Microscopy Channels

Set channel names, excitation/emission wavelength ranges (nm), and custom colors. Editing the emission range automatically updates the color picker to the physical visible light spectrum of the midpoint!

Combination Scoring Weightage & Priorities

Adjust the priority and penalty weights used by SpectraPanel's algorithm to rank generated panel combinations.

40

Reward weight for panels covering 100% of your requested targets.

20

Bonus for secondaries pre-adsorbed against the other host species in the panel.

15

Bonus for distributing antibodies across distinct animal host species per channel.

30

Points deducted when reagents require contradictory fixatives (e.g. Methanol vs PFA).

35

Points deducted when reagents share the same detection channel (e.g. DAPI + Blue FP).

Sync Inventory from Google Sheets

Connect a live Google Sheet workbook to load and synchronize your antibody and reagent inventory directly into SpectraPanel.

Google Sheet Formatting

Ensure your Google Sheet contains the standard tabs: Primaries, Secondaries, Direct_Dyes, and Reporters (matching our downloadable Excel template).

Grant View Access

In Google Sheets, click Share (top-right) → under General Access, change to "Anyone with the link" (Viewer) → click Copy link.

Paste & Sync

Paste the link into the box above and click Sync & Load Inventory. SpectraPanel will fetch all sheets directly via HTTPS without sending any data to external third-party servers.
